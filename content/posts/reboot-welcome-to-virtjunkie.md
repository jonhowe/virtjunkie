It’s been 4 years since my last post – wow!

Welcome to VirtJunkie! As the name implies, I want a place to write about things I’m looking at or am interested in relating to Virtualization (and automation).

A lot has changed for me since my last post. Shortly after my last post,  I started working for a large Swiss bank that prohibited me from contributing to a blog. I left that company and spent 3 years at [Solarwinds](http://www.solarwinds.com/) where I *should*have been contributing to this blog more, but I didn’t. It’s my goal here to provide high quality, relevant content.

Anyhow, I’m back! I’ve got a new domain, a new job (I work for [PCM](http://www.pcm.com/)as a solutions architect) where I specialize in Core Datacenter technology.

Stay tuned – I’m about to be much more active here.
