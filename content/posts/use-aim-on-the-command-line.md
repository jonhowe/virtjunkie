I get bored a lot. There are up sides to this and down sides to this. For some reason I decided that I wanted to find a command llne (CLI) AOL Instant Messenger (AIM) client. I found one that works great called [BSFlite](http://bsflite.sourceforge.net/).

You an see an image of what it looks like at their site [Here](http://bsflite.sourceforge.net/bsflitep9.png).

There aren’t any binaries for Debian or Fedora, but compiling from source was easy. Just **“make”** it and then copy the compiled binary to a directory like **“/usr/local/bin”**.

Give It a shot!
