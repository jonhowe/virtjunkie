<p>My name is Jon Howe, and I love technology! I’ve been fortunate enough to work with some of the most gifted engineers at some of the largest organizations in the world, start ups, and many different verticals. I currently work for a large technology solution provider as a senior architect. I spend my days helping organizations solve intricate challenges based on my experiences and training.</p>
<p>I build relationships with clients and collaborate on solutions. This allows me to best position an array of products and services across the Enterprise. I’ve been fortunate enough to work in a wide variety of industries, including a Fortune 500 investment firm, a company on the Forbes top 50 largest private companies in the US, as well as experience in both medical and IT Software. Using this experience, I quickly create connections that deliver business justifications to executives, as well as technical validation to engineers. Officially, I specialize in “Core Datacenter Technology”, but I tend to lead projects that involve Hybrid/Cloud Native infrastructure, as well as large migrations. I utilize my extensive background in automation and orchestration to give customers a competitive edge.</p>
<p>With over 15 years of experience, I’ve led teams for multiple large international enterprise environments as well as small regionally dispersed teams.</p>
